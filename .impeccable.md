## Design Context

### Users
Cryo is used primarily by data-platform engineers, lakehouse operators, and technical owners
responsible for large Apache Iceberg fleets. They work in a high-consequence operational context:
diagnosing table health, prioritizing maintenance, previewing cost and load, applying policies in
bulk, and monitoring destructive or expensive maintenance safely.

### Brand Personality
Bold, precise, and trustworthy. The product should communicate technical authority and make complex
maintenance decisions feel controlled. It should feel category-defining rather than like a generic
enterprise admin dashboard, without becoming playful or visually noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use an industrial control-plane aesthetic: dense but legible data surfaces, strong information
hierarchy, cool neutral foundations, and a distinctive Cryo visual signature. Retain light and dark
themes and use saturated color only for action, selection, risk, and system state. Avoid generic
card grids, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, neon-on-dark styling, and templated AI-dashboard
patterns.

### Design Principles
1. Make the safest, highest-value next action obvious.
2. Reveal operational complexity progressively while preserving expert efficiency.
3. Turn Cryo's unique intelligence—health, projected savings, load-aware admission, and plan
   verification—into visible decision support rather than isolated metrics.
4. Use a distinctive visual system without compromising trust, density, or accessibility.
5. Explain risk, uncertainty, and system decisions in concrete operator language.
